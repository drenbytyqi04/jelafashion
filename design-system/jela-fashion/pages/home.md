# Home — overrides

Flow: DESIRE (1–3) → COLLECTION (4–5) → PERFECT FIT (6) → TRUST (7–10) → ORDER (11–13).

## Overrides

- The home page is the only page with the intro loader and the transparent nav state.
- The one ticked (tape-measure) divider on this page sits at the top of section 6.
- Surfaces in order: hero (video) · ivory · ivory · ivory · linen · **ivory with ticks** ·
  ink-free full-bleed lookbook · ivory · linen · blush · ivory · image · linen footer.
  Blush appears once (consultation).
- Parallax allowed on: bridal feature large image, atelier story image.
